7 Signs Your Marriage Could Use a Christian Marriage Coach

By Terrie Beeman · October 19, 2026

Asking for help is not a sign your marriage is failing. It is a sign you are serious about winning. Here are seven signs a Christian marriage coach could help.

1. You keep having the same argument

The topic changes, but the pattern doesn't. A coach helps you see the cycle and break it.

2. You feel more like roommates than partners

Busy schedules, kids and careers can slowly push intimacy and friendship aside.

3. Trust has been broken

Infidelity, secrets, or financial betrayal need a guided path to rebuild, not just time.

4. One of you has mentioned divorce

That word changes the atmosphere of a home. Get support before decisions are made in pain.

5. You're standing alone

If your spouse isn't ready, you can still get coaching for yourself and keep your marriage covered in prayer.

6. You're entering a new season

Newlyweds, new babies, empty nests and blended families all bring new pressures.

7. You've stopped praying together

Spiritual distance often comes before emotional distance.
