Does the Bible Allow Divorce for Adultery? (Matthew 19:9 Explained)

By Terrie Beeman · October 25, 2026

Part of our guide: Healing After Infidelity

When a husband cheats, many Christian wives ask the same thing: Am I allowed to divorce him? And if I stay, am I foolish? Scripture speaks to both questions, and it gives more freedom and more hope than many women expect.

What Jesus said

"And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery."Matthew 19:9 (KJV)

The Greek word often translated "fornication" or "sexual immorality" is porneia, a broad word for sexual sin. Jesus named it as an exception: divorce in that case is not sin for the betrayed spouse.

Permitted is not commanded

In the same conversation, Jesus explained that Moses allowed divorce "because of the hardness of your hearts" (Matthew 19:8). Divorce after adultery is permitted. It is never required. Many believers choose to forgive and rebuild when there is true repentance, and that is just as biblical.

Other passages to know

  • Malachi 2:16. God's heart grieves over broken covenants.
  • 1 Corinthians 7:10-15. Paul encourages reconciliation and addresses an unbelieving spouse who leaves.
  • Hosea. A prophet pursues an unfaithful wife as a picture of God's covenant love.

Christians and churches differ on how they apply these passages. Talk with your pastor about how your church understands them.

Questions to pray through

  1. Has the affair truly ended?
  2. Is my husband showing repentance, or only regret?
  3. Am I and my children safe?
  4. What is God saying to me when I quiet the other voices?
  5. Have I sought godly counsel from people who care about both me and my marriage?

For more help, read Should I Stay After He Cheated?.

A word about abuse

Matthew 19 is about adultery. If you are facing abuse, threats or control, that is a safety issue first. God does not ask you to remain in danger. Read When Standing Isn't Safe.

Frequently asked questions

Is divorce a sin if my husband cheated?

According to Matthew 19:9, Jesus named sexual immorality as an exception, so divorce in that case is not sin for the betrayed spouse. Forgiving and rebuilding is also a faithful choice.

Does God want me to stay with a cheating husband?

God values covenant and restoration, but Scripture does not require a betrayed spouse to stay. Pray, seek godly counsel, and look for genuine repentance and safety.

What does porneia mean in Matthew 19:9?

Porneia is a broad Greek word for sexual immorality. Many scholars understand it to include adultery and other sexual sin outside marriage.
