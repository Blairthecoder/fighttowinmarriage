Break the Curse: 20 Declarations to Break Generational Curses Off Your Marriage

By Terrie Beeman · October 19, 2026

Some of the battles in your marriage didn't start with you. Patterns like divorce, infidelity, anger, addiction and silence can be passed down from one generation to the next until someone stands up and says, "It ends with us." In this prayer, Terrie Beeman leads husbands and wives in breaking generational curses off their marriage by the power of the almighty God and in the name of Jesus.

Watch "Break the Curse" on YouTube and pray along with Terrie.

The message: Christ has already broken the curse

"Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us" (Galatians 3:13). You are not bound to repeat what you saw growing up. In Christ, you and your spouse are a new creation (2 Corinthians 5:17), and your marriage can be transformed by the renewing of your mind (Romans 12:2). These declarations put that truth into words over your home.

How to pray these declarations

  • Pray out loud, together if you can. If your spouse isn't ready, stand in the gap and declare them over your marriage yourself.
  • Pray them all, then linger on the ones that hit home. Repeat a declaration until your faith rises to meet it.
  • Pair them with action. Breaking a curse in prayer and building new habits go together. Forgive, set boundaries, get help for addiction, and learn to communicate.

20 declarations to break generational curses off your marriage

  1. Divorce and broken marriages. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of divorce, broken marriages and unhealthy relationships in my family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  2. Infidelity. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of infidelity, adultery and unfaithfulness in my family bloodline. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  3. Abandonment. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of abandonment. Fathers and mothers will not leave their spouses and children. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  4. Abuse. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of domestic violence, whether physical, verbal, emotional or sexual abuse. These evil behaviors will not be normalized in our marriage or our family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  5. Addiction. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of addiction to alcohol, drugs, gambling, pornography or anything else damaging the marriages in my family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  6. Anger. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of chronic anger, explosive tempers, harsh words and unresolved conflict in our marriage. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  7. Unforgiveness. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of unforgiveness. We will no longer hold grudges in our family. Instead, we will pursue reconciliation. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  8. Control and manipulation. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of control and manipulation in our family. We will serve one another in love. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  9. Pride. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of pride in our family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  10. Financial dysfunction. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of financial dysfunction in our family. No more debt and no more greed. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  11. Sexual sin. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of sexual immorality, promiscuity, pornography or any other sexual sin affecting marital intimacy in our family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  12. Poor communication. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of poor communication in our family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  13. Emotional neglect. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of emotional neglect. We will no longer withhold love, affection, encouragement or support from one another. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  14. Fear of commitment. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of fear of commitment in my family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  15. Distrust and jealousy. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of distrust, dishonesty and jealousy. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  16. Codependency. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of codependency and unhealthy emotional dependence in our family. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  17. Unhealthy family boundaries. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of unhealthy family boundaries. We will no longer allow our parents, siblings, relatives or even friends to interfere with our marriage relationship. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  18. Spiritual apathy. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of spiritual apathy. We will no longer neglect prayer, worship or the Word of God in our marriage, leaving it spiritually vulnerable. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  19. Idolatry. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of occult involvement and idolatry. In the mighty name of Jesus.
  20. Victim mentality. By the power of the almighty God, i break the generational curse of a victim mentality. In the mighty name of Jesus.

If you or your children are experiencing abuse, please get safe first. Call 911 or the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233. Prayer and safety go together.

Declare a new legacy

I declare and I decree that my spouse and I are in Christ, and we are a new creation. We are transformed by the renewing of our minds. Lord Jesus, thank You that You redeemed us from every curse by becoming a curse for us, so we are no longer bound by generational curses or curses we have brought on ourselves. As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. In Jesus' mighty name, amen.Galatians 3:13 · 2 Corinthians 5:17 · Romans 12:2 · Joshua 24:15
