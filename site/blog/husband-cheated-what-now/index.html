My Husband Cheated. Now What? A Christian Wife's First 30 Days

By Terrie Beeman · October 25, 2026

Part of our guide: Healing After Infidelity

If you are reading this in the middle of the night with a pounding heart, take a breath. You just found out your husband cheated, and nothing feels real. You don't need to have answers tonight. You only need the next right step, and God will meet you in it.

"The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit."Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

Days 1 to 3: get steady

  • Your safety comes first. If you are afraid of your husband, read When Standing Isn't Safe and reach out for help today.
  • Take care of your body. Drink water, eat something small, and try to rest. Shock is physical.
  • Protect your children. Keep routines as normal as you can and keep adult details away from little ears.
  • Call one safe person. A mature believer, pastor or coach who will pray with you, not someone who will broadcast your pain.
  • Don't post about it. Social media feels like relief for a moment and leaves a mark for years.

Days 4 to 10: get the truth and get help

You need enough truth to make wise decisions. That usually means knowing whether the affair has ended, how long it lasted and who knows. Hunting for every detail at 2:00 AM rarely brings peace, so ask for what you need to know and let the rest come in a safer setting, with a pastor, counselor or coach.

See your doctor and get tested. It is a hard step, and it is an act of caring for yourself.

Write down what you are feeling and bring it to God. The Psalms are full of honest anger and grief. He is not offended by yours.

Days 11 to 20: don't decide forever yet

Everyone will have an opinion. Some will tell you to leave tonight. Some will tell you to forgive and move on by Sunday. Neither is your job right now. Your job is to decide your next step, not your forever.

Ask two questions: Is he repentant and is the affair over? and Am I safe? Those answers shape what restoration could look like. Read Can a Marriage Survive Infidelity? when you are ready.

Days 21 to 30: build a plan

  1. Set clear boundaries. For example: no contact with the affair partner, full transparency with devices, and counseling or coaching.
  2. Get a prayer covering. Join the free Tuesday marriage prayer call so you're not carrying this alone.
  3. Start your own healing. Whether your husband joins or not, you deserve care. Learn how to quiet the replays.
  4. Learn the difference between forgiveness and trust. Read Forgiveness vs. Trust After an Affair.

You are not the reason he cheated

Every marriage has weaknesses, and both spouses may have work to do. But an affair is a choice the unfaithful spouse made. You can look honestly at your marriage without carrying the guilt of his decision.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do right after finding out my husband cheated?

Focus on safety, rest and support. Tell one trusted, mature person, see your doctor, keep routines steady for your children, and avoid making permanent decisions in the first days.

Should I confront the other woman?

Usually not. Confrontation rarely brings the peace you hope for and can escalate the situation. Your energy is better spent on your healing, your safety and clear boundaries with your husband.

Should I leave my husband after he cheated?

You don't have to decide right away. Take time to learn whether he is repentant and the affair has ended, seek godly counsel, and pray. Both restoration and separation are paths some Christian women take.
