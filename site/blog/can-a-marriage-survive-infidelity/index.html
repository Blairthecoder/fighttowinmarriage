Can a Marriage Survive Infidelity? What Scripture and Real Couples Say

By Terrie Beeman · October 25, 2026

Part of our guide: Healing After Infidelity

It is the question that keeps you up at night: Can our marriage really survive this? The honest answer is yes. Many marriages survive infidelity, and some become deeper and more honest than they were before. But survival doesn't happen by accident, and it doesn't happen by pretending.

What Scripture shows us

The book of Hosea tells of a husband who kept pursuing an unfaithful wife as a picture of God's relentless love for His people. That story is not a command that every betrayed spouse must stay. It is a picture of what God's grace can do when hearts turn back to Him.

"Therefore what God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."Mark 10:9 (KJV)

God values covenant. He also values truth, repentance and the safety of His daughters. Restoration holds all of these together.

What restoration requires from the spouse who cheated

  • The affair ends completely. No contact, no "closure" conversations, no friendship.
  • Honesty without trickle truth. Facts revealed a little at a time reopen the wound again and again.
  • Ownership without excuses. "I chose this" instead of "you pushed me to it."
  • Patience with your healing. He doesn't get to set the timeline for your trust.
  • Accountability. A pastor, mentor, coach or group who will ask hard questions.

What restoration asks of the betrayed spouse

None of this is a burden to carry today. These are things God grows in you over time:

  • A willingness to let God heal you, even while you are still angry.
  • Honesty about your pain instead of acting "fine."
  • Openness to looking at the marriage as a whole, without taking the blame for his choice.
  • A growing decision to forgive, which is different from instantly trusting. Read Forgiveness vs. Trust After an Affair.

Signs of real repentance

Look for fruit, not just words: transparency without being asked, consistency over months, empathy for your pain, and a renewed relationship with God. Remorse says "I'm sorry I got caught." Repentance says "I'm sorry I hurt you and God, and I will do whatever it takes."

Signs it may not be safe to rebuild yet

If the affair continues, the lies continue, or there is abuse, threats or control, restoration is not possible right now. That's not a failure of your faith. Read Should I Stay After He Cheated? and When Standing Isn't Safe.

A restored marriage tells a story

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of marriages survive infidelity?

Studies vary widely, but a substantial share of couples who face infidelity choose to stay together, and many report a stronger marriage after doing the work of repair. Repentance, honesty and support make the biggest difference.

Does the Bible say I have to stay after an affair?

No. Jesus permitted divorce for sexual immorality in Matthew 19:9 but never commanded it. Staying and working toward restoration is a biblical choice when it is safe and the unfaithful spouse is repentant.

Can a marriage be better after infidelity?

Many couples say yes. Rebuilding forces honest conversations and new habits that weren't there before. Better does not mean painless, but it is possible.
