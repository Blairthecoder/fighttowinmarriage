Marriage Coaching FAQ

Is this Christian marriage counseling?

Terrie is a Certified Christian Counselor and Marriage Coach. Sessions are faith-based coaching and Christian counseling, not licensed clinical therapy. Coaching uses biblical principles, prayer and practical tools to help you move forward. If you need clinical or crisis care, Terrie will encourage you to seek a licensed professional alongside coaching.

Can I do coaching if my spouse won't join?

Yes. Many people begin coaching alone while standing for their marriage. Individual sessions focus on your heart, your prayer life and the changes you can make.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Sessions are held online by video so couples anywhere can participate.

How much does marriage coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the package. Terrie shares pricing on your free consultation call so you can choose the right fit.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share in coaching stays private, except where safety or the law requires otherwise.
