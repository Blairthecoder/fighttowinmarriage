Fight for Someone Else's Marriage

November 17, 2026 · Speaker: Terrie Beeman, Fight to Win Marriages · Edited recap, not a verbatim transcript

"Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ."Galatians 6:2 (NKJV)

Theme: Intercessory Prayer for Other Couples

  • Praying for other couples
  • Intercessory prayer
  • Bearing one another's burdens

Session overview

Terrie Beeman challenged participants to carry another couple's burden through focused prayer. Rather than waiting for their own marriage to become trouble-free, husbands and wives were encouraged to trust God with their concerns, choose one struggling marriage, and intercede for that couple for one week with urgency, compassion, wisdom, and confidentiality.

Five key teachings

  1. Healthy marriages need prayer too. Fight to Win Marriages welcomes couples in every season and strengthens a spiritual "immune system" through prayer, fasting, Scripture, teaching, and community.
  2. We are called to carry burdens. Galatians 6:2 connects practical intercession with the law of Christ: loving our neighbor as ourselves.
  3. Pray with personal urgency. Choose a couple and pray for their marriage with the same care, persistence, and faith you would want someone to bring to your own.
  4. Protect the couple's dignity. Ask permission when appropriate, learn their prayer needs, keep private details confidential, and avoid gossip or public discussion.
  5. Trust God with your own marriage. Surrender your concerns while you stand in the gap for another couple, believing God can work in both marriages at once.

The one-week prayer challenge

Pray daily through November 24, then return to the Tuesday call prepared to share how God led you.

  • Choose: Ask the Holy Spirit to place one struggling marriage on your heart.
  • Connect: Privately let the couple know you are praying and ask whether they have specific requests.
  • Cover: Pray daily for each spouse, their relationship with God, their covenant, home, family, and future.
  • Confront: Pray against strongholds, trauma, parental wounds, abandonment, secrecy, adultery, abuse, and destructive generational patterns.
  • Declare: Speak Scripture, healing, freedom, wisdom, unity, restoration, and victory over the marriage.
  • Report: Be ready next Tuesday to share how God led you, without exposing confidential details.

Personal Reflection Questions

  1. When was the last time I prayed earnestly for someone else's marriage?
  2. Which couple may need quiet, faithful support this week?
  3. Can I intercede without judging, fixing, gossiping, or taking sides?
  4. What Scripture can I consistently declare over this couple?

Prayer Focus and Next Steps

  • Pray for healing, renewed trust, honest communication, forgiveness, wisdom, and restored commitment.
  • Pray for each spouse to grow closer to God and live as a kingdom husband or kingdom wife.
  • Ask God to expose and break destructive cycles while healing childhood trauma, parental wounds, and unresolved pain.
  • Continue the Tuesday water-only fast until 3:00 p.m., as able, and join prayer at 6:00 p.m. Central.
  • Protect safety: prayer support should never replace emergency help, professional care, or appropriate intervention where abuse or danger is present.
Lord, place a marriage on my heart and teach me how to stand in the gap with love, wisdom, and discretion. Heal every wounded place, break destructive patterns, draw each spouse closer to You, and strengthen their covenant. As I care for another couple, help me trust You with my own marriage. In Jesus' name, amen.

A note on safety: Faithfulness never requires tolerating abuse, and prayer never replaces emergency help. If someone is in danger, help them get safe first: call 911 or the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233.
