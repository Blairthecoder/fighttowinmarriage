Just Thank Him

November 24, 2026 · Speaker: Terrie Beeman, Fight to Win Marriages · Edited recap, not a verbatim transcript

"I will bless the Lord at all times; His praise shall continually be in my mouth."Psalm 34:1 (NKJV)

Supporting Scriptures

  • Romans 8:28: God can work every part of the story together for good
  • Psalm 37:23: Trust God to order and establish your steps
  • Matthew 19:26: What is impossible with people is possible with God
  • Ephesians 3:20: God is able to do beyond what we ask or imagine

Theme: Praise and Gratitude While You Wait

  • Praise while waiting
  • Marriage restoration
  • Faith through delays
  • Gratitude

Download November 24 Prayer Call Notes (PDF)

Session overview

Terrie Beeman encouraged participants to make gratitude a lifestyle, not a holiday moment. When circumstances have not changed and the answer seems delayed, believers can praise God for who He is, remember His record of faithfulness, and thank Him by faith for what they are trusting Him to do in their lives and marriages.

Five key teachings

  1. Praise continually, not conditionally. Psalm 34:1 calls us to bless the Lord at all times. Praise is anchored in God's goodness, not in whether the present moment feels good.
  2. Thank God before the answer appears. Faith-filled gratitude can begin while you are still waiting for healing, restoration, salvation, direction, or renewed trust.
  3. Answer doubt with truth. When fear asks whether God really said it or whether a marriage can be healed, return to Scripture and God's proven faithfulness.
  4. Use the tools God has given you. Prayer, fasting, Scripture, declarations, wise community, and consistent application strengthen a marriage far beyond simply hearing another lesson.
  5. Trust God's process and timing. God may be preparing both the blessing and the people who will carry it. Even confusing seasons can become part of what He works together for good.

When you find yourself asking, 'When, Lord?'

  • Pause: Notice the fear, impatience, or discouragement beneath the question.
  • Remember: Recall what God has already done and what His Word says is possible.
  • Thank: Express gratitude for God's character, presence, and promised care before circumstances change.
  • Declare: Speak truth over your heart, spouse, home, and covenant rather than repeating fear.
  • Obey: Take the next wise, loving, and safe step while leaving the outcome and timing with God.

Personal Reflection Questions

  1. Has my praise become dependent on what I see today?
  2. Which promise or past answer can I remember when doubt repeats itself?
  3. What am I ready to thank God for by faith?
  4. How can I apply prayer, Scripture, wise counsel, and healthy action this week?

Prayer Focus and Next Steps

  • Thank God for who He is and for the good He has already done in your life and marriage.
  • Pray by faith for healing, peace, honesty, trust, love, wisdom, and restoration where those things have been wounded or lost.
  • Ask God to strengthen your faith, quiet repeated suggestions of fear, and help you keep your attention on His truth.
  • Continue standing in the gap for another marriage with compassion, discretion, and confidentiality.
  • Choose wise support. Prayer can work alongside pastoral care, licensed counseling, and practical help.
Lord, I praise You because You are good, faithful, and worthy at all times. Thank You for what You have already done and for the work I cannot yet see. Restore peace, love, honesty, trust, wisdom, and healing in our lives and marriages. When fear asks whether You can do it, help us remember Your Word and Your record. Prepare us for Your answer, guide our next steps, and teach us to trust Your timing and Your way. In Jesus' name, amen.

A note on safety: Healthy boundaries and safety remain important; faithfulness never requires tolerating abuse. If anyone is in danger, seek emergency help and appropriate professional support.
