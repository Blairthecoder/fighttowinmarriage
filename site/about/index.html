About the founder

Meet Terrie Beeman

Certified Christian Counselor, Spiritual Life Coach, Marriage Coach, Motivator and Prayer Warrior, and founder of Fight to Win Marriages.

Why Fight to Win Marriages exists

Terrie Beeman started Fight to Win Marriages with one message: don't give up. Marriage is one of God's greatest institutions, and it is worth fighting for. Too many couples walk away from a covenant God can still heal. Through coaching, prayer and teaching, Terrie helps husbands and wives stop fighting each other and start fighting for each other.

The ministry is founded on 2 Corinthians 10:4 and Ephesians 6:12. Our battle is not against our spouse but against spiritual forces, and God has given us weapons with divine power to pull down strongholds. Fight to Win Marriages partners with God to equip husbands and wives with those weapons, along with biblical principles, prayer, encouragement and practical tools to build strong, healthy, God-centered marriages.

Our vision

To see marriages healed, healthy and whole, and to decrease the divorce rate in the body of believers. Terrie is committed to inspiring faith, restoring hope and putting marriages back on the map.

Who we serve

Married and engaged couples, individuals preparing for marriage, and anyone seeking healing, restoration, wisdom and biblical guidance for their marriage.

What Terrie believes about marriage

  • Marriage is a covenant, and God is in the business of restoration.
  • Your spouse is not your enemy. The battle is spiritual, and it is won in prayer.
  • Healing comes through honesty, forgiveness, faith and practical change.
  • No season is too late, whether you are newlyweds or married 40 years.
