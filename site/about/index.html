About the founder

Meet Terrie Beeman

Biblical marriage coach, intercessor and speaker, and founder of Fight to Win Marriages.

Why Fight to Win Marriages exists

Terrie Beeman started Fight to Win Marriages with one message: don't give up. Too many couples walk away from a covenant God can still heal. Through coaching, prayer and teaching, Terrie helps husbands and wives stop fighting each other and start fighting for each other.

[Terrie's story in her own words: how God led her into marriage ministry, her own marriage journey, years in ministry]

What Terrie believes about marriage

  • Marriage is a covenant, and God is in the business of restoration.
  • Your spouse is not your enemy. The battle is spiritual, and it is won in prayer.
  • Healing comes through honesty, forgiveness, faith and practical change.
  • No season is too late, whether you are newlyweds or married 40 years.
