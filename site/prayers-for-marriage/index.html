Prayers for your marriage when words won't come

Some nights the only thing you can do for your marriage is pray, and some nights you can't even find the words. These prayers are here for both.

A prayer for your marriage tonight

"Father, I bring my marriage to You. You joined us together, and I ask You to guard what You have joined. Heal what is broken in me and in my husband. Silence every voice that isn't Yours. Give me wisdom to know my part and grace to do it. Restore what the enemy has stolen, and let our marriage bring You glory. In Jesus' name, amen."

Prayer and fasting

Some breakthroughs come only through prayer and fasting. The Fight to Win community fasts on Tuesdays (many until 3:00 PM) and prays together that evening. Learn how in Praying and Fasting for Your Marriage. Talk to your doctor first if you have health conditions.

Pray with others every Tuesday

Every Tuesday at 6:00 PM CT, Terrie Beeman leads a free marriage prayer call on Zoom. Wives standing alone, couples, newlyweds and long-married spouses all join. Join the Tuesday prayer call or read the weekly call notes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pray for my marriage when I'm hurt and angry?

Start honestly. Tell God exactly how you feel, then ask Him for what you cannot produce yourself: a soft heart, wisdom and healing. Praying Scripture helps when your own words run out.

What should I pray for my husband?

Pray for his relationship with God, his mind and emotions, protection from temptation, wisdom in his decisions, and a softened heart toward God and toward you.

Is there a group that prays for marriages?

Yes. Fight to Win Marriages hosts a free marriage prayer call every Tuesday at 6:00 PM CT on Zoom.
