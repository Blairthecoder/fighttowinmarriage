Our mission

Putting marriages back on the map

Marriage is one of God's greatest institutions, and we believe it is worth fighting for. Founded on 2 Corinthians 10:4 and Ephesians 6:12, Fight to Win Marriages partners with God to equip husbands and wives with spiritual warfare weapons, biblical principles, prayer, encouragement and practical tools to build strong, healthy, God-centered marriages.

Our vision is to see marriages healed, healthy and whole, and to decrease the divorce rate in the body of believers.

Who we serve

  • Married couples, newlyweds to decades in
  • Engaged couples and individuals preparing for marriage
  • Spouses seeking healing, restoration, wisdom and biblical guidance

Whether your marriage needs healing, strengthening, encouragement or prayer, you are welcome here.

"The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world. On the contrary, they have divine power to demolish strongholds."2 Corinthians 10:4
"For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this dark world and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms."Ephesians 6:12

Winning means winning as a team

In a marriage, your spouse is not the enemy. Most conflict is a battle against stress, exhaustion, old wounds and spiritual opposition. Fighting to win means you team up against the problem and stand together in prayer.

  • Shift your perspective. See the real battle behind the argument.
  • Attack the problem, not the person. Trade "street fighting" for healthy, honoring boundaries.
  • Seek the win-win. If one spouse wins and the other loses, the marriage loses.
  • Stand on the Word. Pray God's promises over your home, even when feelings fade.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fight to Win Marriages?

Fight to Win Marriages is a faith-based marriage ministry led by Terrie Beeman. It encourages couples not to give up on their marriage and focuses on healing, restoration, and standing on God's promises through prayer, love and faith.

Who is marriage coaching for?

Coaching is for married couples, engaged couples, and individual spouses standing for their marriage, whether you are newlyweds or have been married for decades.

When is the Tuesday prayer call?

Terrie hosts an online marriage prayer and fasting call on Zoom every Tuesday. Couples young and old are welcome to join for prayer, encouragement and biblical guidance.

Can I book Terrie to speak at my church or event?

Yes. Terrie speaks at church marriage ministries, couples retreats, women's conferences and marriage events. Use the booking form to share your date, location and audience.
