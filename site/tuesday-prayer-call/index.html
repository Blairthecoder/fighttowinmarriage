What happens on the call

  • Corporate prayer over marriages, homes and families.
  • A word of encouragement from Scripture for the week ahead.
  • Biblical guidance for real questions couples are facing.
  • Prayer requests lifted up by name, as you are comfortable.

Why prayer and fasting?

Some battles are only won on our knees. Terrie learned that firsthand during her own 365-day fight for her marriage, when prayer, fasting and the voice of the Holy Spirit carried her through. Fasting humbles the heart and sharpens our focus on God. Terrie fasts each Tuesday for the couples on the call, so you never have to stand alone.

Tuesday Prayer Call FAQ

Is the Tuesday prayer call free?

Yes. The call is free and open to couples and individuals who want prayer for their marriage. Join on Zoom every Tuesday at 6:00 PM CT using the link on this page, meeting ID 840 8334 7510.

Do both spouses need to attend?

No. Come together or come alone. Many people join while standing in prayer for a spouse who is not ready yet.

Do I have to fast to join?

No. Terrie prays and fasts for the marriages on the call. You are welcome to fast with her as you feel led, but it is not required to attend.

Do I have to be on camera or speak?

No. You can listen, pray along and type requests privately if you prefer.

Can I submit a prayer request if I can't attend?

Yes. Use the prayer request form on this page and Terrie will lift your marriage up in prayer.
